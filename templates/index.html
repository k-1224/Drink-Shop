About Nomolicious

Nomolicious is an online drink shop created for people who enjoy a variety of refreshing and delicious beverages. Our website offers a selection of milk teas, lattes, and fruit teas, with different flavours and customization options to suit individual preferences. Customers can explore the menu, choose their preferred drink sizes and toppings, add items to their cart, and place their orders conveniently through the website. Our goal is to provide a simple, enjoyable, and user-friendly ordering experience where customers can easily discover and customise their favourite drinks.

Nomolicious offers a variety of non-alcoholic drinks, including milk teas, lattes, and fruit teas. There are also different flavours and variations available, including several matcha options.

You can click the account icon in the navigation bar to view information about your recent orders, including the order details and expected arrival information.
